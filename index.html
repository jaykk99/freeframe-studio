Is it really free?

Yes. Images come from the open Pollinations.ai API, which needs no key or account. Motion clips render on your own device, so they cost nothing at all.

Why do I sometimes wait between images?

The free image API allows one image every 45 seconds per visitor. FreeFrame queues your requests automatically and shows the countdown.

Is the video "real" AI video?

Not quite. Free video models don't have open no-key APIs, so FreeFrame animates your image with cinematic camera moves rendered in-browser. It's great for reels, intros and storyboards.

Where are my creations saved?

In your browser's local library on this device. Download anything you want to keep for good.
