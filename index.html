Is it really free?

Yes. Images and AI text come from the open Pollinations.ai API (no key, no account). Background removal, upscaling, 3D depth, editing and video all run on your own device.

What does Pro unlock?

Pro is a one-time $19 purchase at Monico Labs. It raises exports from 1024px to 2048px on the long edge and adds a commercial-use licence for what you make. Everything else stays free, with no sign-up.

Why is there a wait between images?

The free API allows one image (and one AI text reply) every 45 seconds per visitor. FreeFrame queues everything and shows a countdown, so you can line up several jobs and walk away.

How does 3D parallax work?

A small depth-estimation AI (Depth Anything) runs in your browser, works out what's near and far in your image, and the camera moves through it. The model downloads once (~27 MB) and is cached.

Is the video "real" AI video?

No generative video model is free without a key, so FreeFrame animates your stills with camera moves and AI depth. Great for reels, intros, storyboards and stories.

Where is my stuff saved?

On this device only (your browser's storage). Download anything you want to keep forever.
